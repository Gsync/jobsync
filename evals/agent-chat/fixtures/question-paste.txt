1. Tell me about a time you disagreed with your manager.
Answer: On a payments migration my manager wanted a big-bang cutover. I laid out the rollback risk with numbers from our last incident, proposed a two-week dual-write phase, and we shipped it that way with no customer impact.

2. How do you prioritise when everything is urgent?
Answer: I sort by customer impact and reversibility, agree the order with whoever owns the roadmap, and say out loud what is being dropped so nobody is surprised later.

3. What is the difference between a process and a thread?
Answer: A process has its own address space; threads share one inside a process. Threads are cheaper to create and switch, but shared memory means you need locks or message passing to stay safe.

4. Why do you want to work at a smaller company?
Answer: I want to own a product area end to end and see the effect of my work on customers within weeks, not quarters.

5. Describe a production incident you led.
Answer: A bad index migration locked our orders table at peak. I called the incident, rolled the migration back, wrote the timeline that night, and added an online-migration check to CI the following week.
